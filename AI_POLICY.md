
## OpenFE Ecosystem Policies

The following policies hold for all repositories in the OpenFE ecosystem:

1. Repository Maintainers always reserve the right to make exceptions to any of the above rules on a case-by-case basis.
2. LLMs use must be always disclosed. At minimum in the PR description, but preferably reflected in the “Authors” of the commit.
3. LLMs may be used for translation (disclosing LLM use is important here, so that maintainers can be more considerate when encountering translation inaccuracies).
5. All content must have a human identified as being accountable for the contribution. No agent-only commits.


## Policies specific to this repository

The following policies are specific only to this repository, and decided by the repository's maintainer(s).

1. ✅️ Generating content that only the author sees, but affects their work (searching repositories, asking questions, finding bugs, self-review, linting).
2. ✅️ Generating code that is shared, but not committed to the code base (e.g. Minimally Reproducible Examples in issue text).
3. Content which the human author has:

|                                                               |Code   |Docs   |PRs & Issues|Replies to humans|
|---------------------------------------------------------------|:-----:|:-----:|:----------:|:---------------:|
|Read thoroughly and understands every line before submission   |   ✅️  |   ✅️   |     ❌     |        ❌       |
|Checked for obvious issues before submission                   |   ❌  |   ❌   |     ❌     |        ❌       |
|Not reviewed before submission                                 |   ❌  |   ❌   |     ❌     |        ❌       |
